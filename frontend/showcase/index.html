Managing SCM BPO Operations in One Connected Workspace

SCM BPO delivers smart supply chain management and business operations. From freight routing and vendor contracts to customer commitments and warehouse fulfillment, SCM BPO unifies its operational ecosystem inside SERP Hawk CRM.

Customer Management

Organizing accounts, contractual tiers, and logistics service agreements across enterprise shippers, regional distributors, and global freight partners.

Sales & Leads

Capturing incoming supply chain inquiries, qualifying shipping volume requirements, and advancing high-value commercial logistics proposals.

Projects

Managing complex multi-phase fulfillment rollouts, carrier onboardings, customs transition schedules, and regional warehouse deployments.

Communication

Consolidating email correspondence, schedule updates, rate confirmations, and dispatch notifications into connected client histories.

Team Operations

Aligning account managers, logistics coordinators, and dispatch directors with clear daily work queues and transparent accountability.

Business Activities

Real-time audit trails of client calls, milestone approvals, carrier check-ins, scheduled reviews, and routine operational transactions.
